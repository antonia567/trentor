The Problem

Selling online in Africa hits a roadblock in the final step: getting the product to the customer.

High Delivery Costs


Own logistics operations or outsourced services increase the final product cost and reduce the shopkeeper's margin.


Rural Zones Poorly Served


Most delivery solutions only cover major urban centers, leaving entire regions unserved.


Lack of Efficiency and Tracking


Manual processes generate delays, lost shipments, and limited visibility into the order until final delivery.


The Market

Companies that sell online and need to deliver.

Who are the customers


Companies with marketplaces, e-commerces and various stores. All these business models are potential customers of TRENTOR.


Market Size


The market is not yet formally dimensioned. It is one of our next steps in validation.


Potential


Our ecosystem can encompass all locations on the continent, giving the market significant growth potential.


We connect shopkeepers and marketplaces to customers across the territory. Less costs, more reach, and every order tracked from start to finish.

The TRENTOR Solution

An integrated logistics operation, designed for marketplaces that need to scale deliveries.

1. Reception

2. Transportation

3. Delivery to Customer


The order arrives at the final customer quickly and reliably, from start to finish.
