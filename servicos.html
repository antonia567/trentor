From the store to the customer's door.

We handle the logistics so you can sell more and worry less about delivery. Designed for e-commerces and marketplaces in Angola.

What we do

  • Reception

    We receive the products directly from the shopkeeper or partner marketplace, ready for shipment.

  • Transport

    We move the orders between centers and delivery zones, starting with urban areas and progressively expanding to rural zones.

  • Delivery

    The order arrives at the final customer quickly and reliably, from start to finish.

  • Tracking for orders

    Less delays and lost shipments, and more visibility on where each order is.

How it works

  1. Deliver us the products

    We combine with you the pickup or delivery.

  2. Transport

    The orders follow the most efficient route to the customer's area.

  3. Delivery

    The delivery is made to the final consumer and you track the result.

For whom it is

  • Online stores that need to deliver in Angola.
  • Marketplaces that want to scale the number of deliveries.
  • Shopkeepers that want to reduce logistics costs and reach more areas.
